About Enterprise Blueprints

Open architecture for the AI-native enterprise.

Enterprise Blueprints develops practical architecture for organizations where humans and AI agents can create applications and compose software capabilities on demand.

Our central idea is Thin Apps. Thick Platforms. Experiences can change quickly. Shared business meaning, authoritative state, enterprise capabilities, identity, and policy need durable foundations.

Public sector first

Public-sector use cases help us test the architecture. The underlying ideas apply across the enterprise.

We begin with core Case Management. We define logical architecture independently of vendors, then use implementations to test it.

A living knowledge library

This site brings together foundations, use cases, entities, capabilities, patterns, policies, blueprints, implementations, and architecture decisions. Draft labels show where the work is still developing.

Start with AI-Native Enterprise, or read the current charter for the initiative’s purpose and principles.

Open and independent

The material is free to read and reuse under the applicable licenses. GitHub holds the source. YouTube will support teaching and demonstrations.

Civgentic is the creator’s separate consulting business. Commercial work does not determine Enterprise Blueprints’ architecture or editorial conclusions.
