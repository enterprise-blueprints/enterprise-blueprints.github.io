# OIG editorial and safety review guidance

- Treat this as an **unofficial educational capability model**, not an official or complete OIG capability taxonomy. Label all sample implementation patterns as illustrative.
- The generic scope is federal Offices of Inspector General in the United States. State, local, international and special statutory offices may have different mandates.
- Never publish a real-world case, agency spotlight, specific vendor implementation, or assumed technology configuration in R1.
- Do not promise anonymous/confidential/protected reporting universally; applicable protections, routing, legal obligations and procedures vary.
- Do not imply that every OIG determines employment retaliation claims. Refer to authorized channels and applicable law.
- Do not solicit disclosures, identifying data, evidence uploads or other sensitive information on the site.
- Display a clear 'Not a reporting channel' notice with an official general-purpose locator for reporting routes: https://www.oversight.gov/where-report-fraud-waste-abuse-or-retaliation
- Source each factual legal/general OIG claim to statutes or primary federal oversight resources, and show the reviewed date. Separate the authors' modeling interpretation from source claims.
- Do not present `UNLICENSED-DEMO` as a final content license; public distribution requires a license decision.
