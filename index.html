An open reference library for the AI-native enterprise

Build Experiences.
Share Capabilities.

As AI makes software easier to create, the enterprise has to provide durable shared information, capabilities, governance, and contracts. Otherwise every new app, workflow, and agent reinvents the organization's responsibilities on its own.

Understand the architecture Explore a mission blueprint

One architecture, many connected views

Enterprise Blueprints describes the enterprise as a single, evolving architecture. Experiences change quickly. The foundation underneath them, meaning the information, capabilities, and governed contracts they rely on, should change deliberately and be shared.

Trust and governance aren't a layer you add at the end. They apply to every part of the architecture.
